## 2024-05-13 - [Performance bottleneck in useAnimatedNumber]
**Learning:** React components using `requestAnimationFrame` for animations (e.g., `useAnimatedNumber`) re-render at 60fps. Ensure any heavy computations or array iterations (like `.filter`) at the component's top level are wrapped in `useMemo` and consolidated into a single-pass loop to prevent massive CPU spikes during the animation lifecycle.
**Action:** Always verify if components with high-frequency re-renders (like those with `requestAnimationFrame`) have complex computations at the top level and ensure they are properly memoized.
